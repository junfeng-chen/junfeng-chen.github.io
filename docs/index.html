About me

I am an Assistant Professor at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST).
Previously, I was a the postdoctoral researcher at Southern University of Science and Technology, hosted by Prof. Kailliang Wu at the Department of Mathematics.
I received my Bachelor degrees in Aerospace Engineering and Mathematics from Tsinghua University in 2016, followed by a Diplôme d'Ingénieur and a PhD from Mines Paris – Université PSL. My PhD supervisors are Prof. Elie Hachem and Jonathan Viquerat.

Research Interests
Reliable, efficient, and robust deep-learning methods for computational science and engineering; data-driven flow modeling; multiscale and chaotic dynamics; dimension reduction and reduced-order modeling.
